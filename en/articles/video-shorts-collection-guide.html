Self-hosted shorts collection: travel, nature, macro & tech motion

2026-06-14 · 中文版

Self-hosted MP4s give you speed, SEO, and branding control. aoglang hosts eight bilingual clip pages (~5–22 sec each). This feature groups them by theme with technical and publishing notes.

Why dedicated clip pages

Unique URLs, posters, duration text, and VideoObject schema—see self-hosted video SEO. Better for long-tail queries than iframe-only embeds.

Travel & nature

Aerial boat on teal coast (~22 sec) and underwater surf (~5 sec). Cross-link Tokyo aerial guide for air-to-water storytelling.

Macro & mood

Blue butterfly macro and warm visual clip (~14 sec) for nature and brand mood boards.

Tech motion

Network globe with world map still; plus tech visual short and HD motion visual.

Compression & CDN

Target ~2–5 Mbps for 1080p shorts under 15 sec; always ship a poster image. See new uploads notes for the build pipeline.

Accessibility

Use controls and playsinline; no loud autoplay. Surround players with descriptive prose for users who cannot see the footage.

Adding a new clip

Drop MP4 in upload/video/, register in VIDEOS, run npm run build, then update this feature or the uploads article.
