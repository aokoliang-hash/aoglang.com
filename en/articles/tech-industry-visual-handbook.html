Tech & industry visual handbook: chips, energy & office scenes

2026-06-15 · 中文版

Tech storytelling needs more than blue circuit backgrounds. This handbook maps semiconductor, energy, space, and desk/news imagery in our gallery to real B2B use cases.

Semiconductor & advanced manufacturing

Wafer and inspection close-ups stress precision and cleanrooms—see wafer inspection 01 through 04 for fab reports and recruiting pages.

Energy & grid visuals

Electric grid lines and Earth from space pair with aerial wind features in Fuzhou & offshore wind for macro-to-infrastructure narratives.

Office, news & devices

Laptop news desk, newspaper top view, and iPhone screen close-up suit media and SaaS stories—distinct from people-focused support shots in the portrait handbook.

Safety, outdoor & lifestyle crossovers

Protective suit portraits, hikers on a summit, and senior relaxing outdoors humanize otherwise cold tech pages.

Abstract backgrounds—use sparingly

Abstract water ripples work for transitions, not as the only visual language of a site.

Alt & meta examples

Good alt: “Wafer chip inspection under microscope in a cleanroom, for semiconductor manufacturing stories.” Skip keyword lists.

Mix with 3D & video

Pair stills with Infinity 3D art and the network globe clip for keynotes.

Index

Chips: 01–04. Energy: grid · Earth.
