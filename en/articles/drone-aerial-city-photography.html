Drone city aerials: Tokyo, Fuzhou & offshore wind

2026-06-01 · 中文版

Aerial visuals are a core part of the aoglang gallery. This guide explains how to write discoverable copy around city and energy themes and link to individual photo pages.

Topics and keywords: from landmarks to industries

Image SEO needs more than filenames. State place + angle + time (e.g. dusk drone view of Tokyo’s waterfront expressway) and use phrases people search for. Compare with pages like Tokyo waterside highway and Fuzhou stadium aerial.

Publishing in sets with internal links

Multiple angles of one city (e.g. highway bridge 01 and 02) work well as a series in an article. Energy shots can link to offshore wind turbines.

Images and performance

Use WebP thumbs on indexes and moderately sized heroes on detail pages. Our build generates -thumb.webp variants—see WebP gallery performance.
