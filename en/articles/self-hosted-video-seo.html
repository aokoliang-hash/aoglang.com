Self-hosted MP4 pages: UX and video SEO

2026-05-29 · 中文版

You do not have to embed every clip from a third-party platform. The video section hosts MP4 examples with dedicated pages.

What each page needs

Provide a unique URL, clear h1 and meta description, a poster image, duration text, and VideoObject JSON-LD—see tech visual short and warm visual clip.

File size

Keep shorts under ~15 seconds with sensible compression; posters prevent layout shift. Host very large files on a CDN and link from the article.

Link galleries and articles

Cross-link related stills such as AI high-tech lights to build topical clusters.
