Tech & lifestyle visuals as searchable topics

2026-05-28 · 中文版

Beyond aerials, the gallery covers tech, work, energy, and people. Articles explain intent and use cases—often easier to rank than image-only URLs.

Group by use case

Office themes: laptop news desk, technician at work. Energy/tech: electric grid lines, wafer inspection.

Lifestyle and emotion

Human moments such as senior relaxing outdoors suit wellness and leisure niches—state the audience in one sentence.

Publishing workflow

After adding uploads, run npm run build, publish an article, feature it on the home page, and let the script refresh search JSON and sitemap.
